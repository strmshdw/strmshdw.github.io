QUALITY OF LIFE // COMMAND CENTER

[ 06A ] DASHBOARD UPGRADES

  • ◆ Between-Matches Command Center Demolished the giant "WAITING FOR KICKOFF" billboard after complaints that it was blocking the Jumbotron. The idle screen now goes straight to your Session KPIs, Core Playlist Ranks (3v3, 2v2, 1v1), FOR vs. AGAINST team stats, and your Last Match Box Score.
  • ◆ Most-Played Playlist Net MMR Session Net MMR now tracks your most-played mode of the session instead of adding 1v1 and 3v3 MMR together like a madman.
  • ◆ Custom Calendar Date Picker Filter your entire dashboard, win/loss records, and stat averages to any custom FROM → TO date range.
  • ◆ 20-Game Hosted Replay Buffer Guard Rocket League's servers only hold a rolling buffer of 20 match replays (which we normally download when you close the game). Play a 21st match without syncing and game #1 is vaporized forever. You can now set a configurable game threshold (e.g. 15 / 20 games) to fire a desktop notification & warning banner, enable Auto-Sync at Threshold to automatically download all pending replays mid-session, or click Sync & Download Now at any time.

BROADCAST TECH DESK // PATCH CHANGELOG

[ 06B ] BUG FIXES & TELEMETRY REPAIRS

8 COMPETITIVE FIXES SHIPPED
  • ◆ Fixed a bug where Diamond I, Diamond II, and Diamond III did not exist. An off-by-three rank table error previously caused Diamond players to be legally classified as Champions and Grand Champions to ascend into the astral plane. All historical ranks have been restored to their rightful tiers.
  • ◆ Fixed a bug where Division I players had their division stripped Players in the first division of any rank were previously told they were simply "Grand Champion I" with no division to their name.
  • ◆ Fixed post-game MMR refusing to update until the next match ended. The app now patiently interrogates the servers for up to 25 seconds after the final whistle until your new MMR is handed over.
  • ◆ Fixed the local player ("YOU") card tracking a head-to-head rivalry against yourself. Your own card now shows your active session performance split cleanly into In Wins vs. In Losses.
  • ◆ Fixed a double-negative bug in Player Match History Beating a rival opponent is no longer celebrated by labeling the match a LOSS.
  • ◆ Fixed a haunted pop-up bug Clicking a player or telemetry button will no longer occasionally open the window and slam it shut on the exact same frame.
  • ◆ Fixed a spiteful text-selection bug inside pop-ups Highlighting text inside a pop-up and releasing the mouse outside the border no longer closes the window out of spite.
  • ◆ Updated the localization files. PATCH NOTES TRADITION
