ARASHIKAGE MONOLITH
DESIGN SYSTEM

Arashikage Monolith (嵐影 · 銀座碑文) is a universal design language founded on carved permanence, orthographic clarity, ceremonial restraint, and mechanical consequence. Born from the synthesis of traditional Japanese woodblock printmaking (木版画), the documentary authority of carved vermilion seals (判子) on hand-laid mulberry scrolls (巻物), and 1920s Tokyo Ginza Geometric Art Deco architecture, the system treats the digital viewport as an engraved architectural monolith.

TENET JAPANESE PRINCIPLE ARCHITECTURAL MANDATE
I. MATERIAL HONESTY 物質の誠実 · Busshitsu Opaque 3-stratum mineral relief & stepped ziggurat rules—zero blur or drop-shadows
II. 90/10 RESTRAINT 九分墨・一分朱 · Kubu Sumi 90% Washi, Sumi & incised hairlines; ≤10% solid Shu-iro vermilion authority seals
III. ORTHOGRAPHIC RIGOR 幾何学的規律 · Kikagaku Razor-sharp 0px right angles, upright tracked display capitals & tabular numerals
IV. SOLID MECHANICS 機構的応答 · Kikōteki Decisive Hanko ink strikes, Shōji track glides & discrete chronograph detents

PILLAR 08 // DESIGN GOVERNANCE & ARCHITECTURAL INTEGRITY

[ 08 ] THE 10 PRINCIPLES OF ARASHIKAGE CRAFT

↗ VIEW MARKDOWN SPECIFICATION
✓ DO (ARASHIKAGE MONOLITH STANDARD)
  • Maintain the 90/10 pigment ratio: 90% Washi, Sumi, and Kintsugi Brass; reserve solid Shu-iro Vermilion (≤10%) strictly for carved Hanko stamps ([嵐影之印]), critical anomalies ([印]), and counterparty slabs (陰).
  • Practice Functional Kanji Restraint: use Kanji only where genuinely functional (Hanko maker seal, subtle background watermark, material expression names, or in place of a standalone icon), keeping secondary 1px mineral cartouches in clean tracked English + geometric glyphs (◆ THRESHOLD, ● VERIFIED).
  • Set monumental headlines in 100% unified Washi Ivory / Sumi Ink so adjacent square Hanko stamps command the focal point.
  • Maintain single-line cartouche strips and row-for-row horizontal symmetry across bilateral comparative diptychs.
  • Use decisive, zero-overshoot motion curves (--ease-hanko-strike, --ease-shoji-enter, steps()).
✕ DON'T (FORBIDDEN ANTI-PATTERNS)
  • Never prepend superfluous decorative Kanji prefixes onto English section headings, tabs, stage numbers, or secondary status pills.
  • Never use saturated neon text on dark surfaces next to vermilion Hanko seals.
  • Never split display headlines into two colors across line breaks or stretch a Hanko seal into a full-width horizontal sentence ribbon.
  • Never nest double-bordered ziggurat frames inside double-bordered frames.
  • Never use optical blur (backdrop-filter), diffused drop shadows, faux-slanted italics, or elastic spring overshoots.
